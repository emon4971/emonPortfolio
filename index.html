A Machine Learning Approach for Predicting Student Engagement with Online Learning Platform

IEEE Xplore · ECCE 2025

Examines engagement patterns of 422 university and college students on platforms like Coursera, Udemy and edX. After SMOTE balancing and chi-squared feature selection, CatBoost reached the highest accuracy, with quiz completions, assignment submissions and discussion participation as key engagement predictors.

Co-authors: Abdullah Al Noman Bhuyan, Md. Abdur Rahman Roky, Md. Firoz Hasan · DIU, Dhaka

Thyroid Cancer Detection and Staging Using Machine Learning

SEER database · 423,849 patients

An ML-based method for quick detection and classification of thyroid cancer, predicting whether a sample indicates cancer and the specific cancer stage. Six supervised models were compared, showing that aggregation-based methods can assist clinical experts in early diagnosis and treatment.
